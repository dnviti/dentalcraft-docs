# DentalCraft documentation

The documentation of [DentalCraft](https://github.com/dnviti/dentalcraft), a desktop application for digital dentistry written in Rust, in English and Italian. Every document is available as a Word file, a PDF and Markdown source.

*DentalCraft is not a certified medical device. Nothing here is clinical advice.*

## Updates

The documentation is regenerated at every release of DentalCraft. The current edition (22 November 2026) describes version 0.2.0-alpha.15 (Aligners module 0.2.0-alpha.13, engine 0.3.0-alpha.9), with removing scans, the bite registration that accepts partial scans, the closed gingiva and the checked tooth numbering, on top of the Settings and the direct printing of aligners. The rule is in the [README](https://github.com/dnviti/dentalcraft-docs#how-this-repository-is-updated).

La documentazione viene rigenerata a ogni release di DentalCraft. L'edizione attuale (22 novembre 2026) descrive la versione 0.2.0-alpha.15 (modulo Allineatori 0.2.0-alpha.13, motore 0.3.0-alpha.9), con la rimozione delle scansioni, la registrazione del morso che accetta scansioni parziali, la gengiva chiusa e la numerazione dei denti controllata, oltre alle Impostazioni e alla stampa diretta degli allineatori.
